Dubai Land Department was established to create a thriving, transparent, and sustainable real estate market in Dubai. As the regulatory authority, DLD oversees all property transactions, ensures investor confidence, and drives innovation across the sector.

From digitizing property registration to pioneering real estate tokenization, DLD leads the transformation of Dubai into a global real estate capital attracting investors from over 175 nationalities.
