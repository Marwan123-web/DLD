Dubai Land Department works through the directives of His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Vice President and Prime Minister of the UAE, Ruler of Dubai, to achieve a vision that places Dubai as the best city in the world to live in. The department is committed to building strong partnerships that enhance collaboration, drive innovation and support the development of Dubai's real estate sector. Through strategic alliances with government entities, private organizations, and international bodies, DLD aims to create a world-class real estate environment that attracts investors, protects the rights of all parties, and fosters sustainable growth. Our partnership approach is guided by principles of transparency, mutual benefit, and a shared commitment to excellence. We work closely with our partners to develop and implement policies, initiatives, and services that advance the goals of Dubai's real estate sector and contribute to the overall prosperity of the emirate. We welcome organizations and institutions that share our vision to apply the forms online for a partnership or communication with the department.
